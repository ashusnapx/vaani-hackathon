# Design

## World: the society gate register

Every Indian apartment building keeps a visitor register at the gate: ruled
paper, printed column heads, entries in carbon-blue ballpoint, a rubber stamp.
Doorman is that register for one person's phone. The live log is the register;
each thing the agent does is an entry; outcomes are stamped.

Rejected: the dark security console (the category's rut) and the soft pastel
care app (its opposite).

## Ground and ink (from Vaani's own site, by request)

The user asked for vaani.dev as inspiration, not a copy: Doorman sits in the
same family as the platform it runs on.

- Paper `#f7f3ec` with Vaani's 24px dot grid; surfaces `#efebe3` / `#ebe4d8`; hairlines `#e6dfd4`.
- Ink `#1a1f24`, muted `#6d675f`, bronze accent `#8c6b45` (hot `#b57940`).
- Primary action: Vaani's dark gradient pill (`#2a3038 → #1a1f24 → #0f1216`).
- Stamps: scam `#a8321f`, safe `#3d6b45`, family `#2c4f8a`, pending `#8a6a1f`. Always paired with a word, never colour alone.
- Light only: it is read in a bright hall and on a laptop beside a speakerphone.

## Type

- Display: Instrument Serif (Vaani's own face), normal and italic.
- Column heads, stamps, labels: Archivo at condensed width, uppercase, tracked.
- Entries and body: Manrope; tabular numerals for times and numbers.

## Signature moment

A rubber stamp lands on the row when Doorman decides: scaled down from 1.6,
rotated into a slight tilt, ink grain from an SVG noise mask. One moment only;
everything else enters by a short fade. Reduced motion: stamps appear in place.

## Structure

Masthead (register cover) → pitch and call-to-action → the register (live
table) → controls (check-in call, rehearsal) → earlier pages (past calls with
recordings).
