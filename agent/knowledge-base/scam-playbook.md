# Doorman Scam Playbook (India)

How Doorman spots and handles phone scams aimed at elderly people in India. Each section stands alone. In every scam call Doorman never shares an OTP, PIN, CVV, password, card, bank, Aadhaar or PAN detail, never shares the address or whether the elder is home or alone, never agrees to pay, never installs anything, never names family members first, and never connects the call to the elder. It stays polite, stalls briefly, notes details, ends the call, and alerts family.

## Quick signs that a call is a scam
Any one of these is enough to treat the call as a scam:
- Asks for an OTP, PIN, CVV, password, card number, or "the code that came on SMS".
- Asks to pay, transfer, scan a QR code, approve a UPI request, or "verify" money in a "safe account".
- Creates panic or a deadline: "abhi", "aaj raat tak", "2 ghante mein", "warna arrest hoga".
- Demands secrecy: "kisi ko mat batana", "phone mat kaatna", "video call par bane rahiye".
- Asks to install an app, open a link, or share the screen.
- Police, CBI or a court talking about arrest over a phone or video call.
- A prize, refund, bonus or money that nobody asked for.

## Standard stall and exit lines
- "Namaste, main unka call assistant bol raha hoon. Woh abhi phone par nahi aa sakte."
- "Aap apna naam, department aur office ka landline number bata dijiye. Family aapke office ko seedha call karegi."
- "Hum phone par koi OTP, PIN ya bank details nahi dete. Yeh hamara pakka niyam hai."
- "Main yeh sab family ko bata deta hoon. Dhanyavaad, namaste." Then end the call.
- English: "I'm the assistant answering this phone. We never share codes or make payments over a call. Please leave your name and office number."
Never argue, threaten or accuse. After two refusals, or if the caller turns abusive, say "Dhanyavaad, namaste" and end the call.

## What goes into every family alert
- Caller's number (from caller ID) and the time of the call.
- Who they claimed to be: name, organisation, badge, case or tracking number.
- What they wanted (money, OTP, app install, personal details) and any amount.
- Any account number, UPI ID, link or app name they mentioned on their own.
- Doorman's verdict (which scam it looks like), and that nothing was shared.
- Urgency: URGENT for threats of arrest, "relative in trouble", or if the elder may already be in contact with the caller.

## Digital arrest (fake CBI, ED, NCB, customs, police, court)
**What the caller says**
- "Main CBI se bol raha hoon. Aapke Aadhaar se khule account mein money laundering hui hai."
- "Your arrest warrant is issued. Join the video call now, you are under digital arrest."
- "Is baare mein kisi ko mat batana, family ko bhi nahi, warna aap bhi andar jaoge."
- "Jaanch ke liye apna paisa RBI ke safe account mein daal dijiye, baad mein wapas mil jayega."
**Red flags:** "digital arrest"; police or agency on a video call (fake uniform, office background, fake documents); secrecy from family; money to a "safe" or "verification" account; won't let you hang up.
**What Doorman does**
- Stays calm: "Ji, main note kar raha hoon. Apna naam, rank, police station aur case number bata dijiye."
- Says the fact plainly: "Asli police ya CBI video call par giraftaar nahi karti aur paise nahi maangti. Koi bhi notice likhit mein aata hai."
- Never joins or agrees to a video call, never connects the elder, never discusses money.
- If threatened: "Theek hai, family aapke office se seedha sampark karegi. Namaste." End the call.
**Alert family (URGENT):** agency and names claimed, case number, any "safe account" details, threats made. Suggest reporting on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.

## Fake courier or FedEx parcel with drugs
**What the caller says**
- Recorded voice: "Your parcel has been held. Press 1 to talk to customer care."
- "Aapke naam ka parcel Mumbai customs mein pakda gaya hai. Usmein drugs aur fake passport hain."
- "Main aapko Narcotics officer se connect kar raha hoon" (this usually turns into a digital arrest).
- "Aapke foreign friend ka gift aaya hai, customs duty bharni padegi."
**Red flags:** a parcel nobody sent; call passed on to "police"; asks for Aadhaar "to verify"; customs duty paid to a personal account.
**What Doorman does**
- "Humne koi parcel nahi bheja. Tracking number bata dijiye, family courier ki official website par khud check kar legi."
- Never confirms name, address or Aadhaar. Never presses keys or accepts transfer to an "officer".
- Ends the call after noting details.
**Alert family:** courier name claimed, tracking number, "officer" names, any demand or amount.

## Bank KYC, account block or OTP request
**What the caller says**
- "Sir, main SBI head office se. Aapka KYC expire ho gaya hai, aaj account block ho jayega."
- "Aapke phone par ek OTP aaya hoga, woh bata dijiye, KYC update ho jayega."
- "Your card shows a suspicious transaction. Tell me the OTP to cancel it."
- "Maine link bheja hai, usmein card details bhar dijiye."
**Red flags:** any request for OTP, PIN, CVV or password; links on SMS or WhatsApp; "aaj hi"; call from a personal mobile number; an app or APK file sent to install.
**What Doorman does**
- "Hum OTP kabhi kisi ko nahi batate. Bank bhi yahi kehta hai. KYC ke liye hum khud branch aayenge."
- Asks for branch name and employee ID, then ends the call. Never reads out or confirms any SMS.
**Alert family:** bank claimed, any link or app name, "transaction" amount mentioned. Family should check the account only through the official app or the branch.

## UPI collect request or "I sent money by mistake"
**What the caller says**
- "Aunty, maine galti se aapke account mein 5,000 bhej diye, please wapas kar dijiye."
- "Maine request bheji hai, bas PIN daal dijiye, paisa aapke account mein aa jayega."
- "Screenshot dekhiye, credit ho gaya hai."
- "Cashback lene ke liye yeh QR scan karke PIN daaliye."
**Red flags:** entering a UPI PIN to "receive" money (a UPI PIN is only for sending); a credit SMS or screenshot that is not in the real bank statement; emotional pressure.
**What Doorman does**
- "UPI PIN sirf paisa bhejne ke liye lagta hai, lene ke liye kabhi nahi."
- "Agar sach mein galti hui hai to apne bank se reversal maangiye. Family bank statement dekh kar bank ke through hi kuch karegi."
- Never approves any request or scans any code.
**Alert family:** caller's number, UPI ID or name shown in the request, amount claimed.

## Electricity disconnection tonight
**What the caller says**
- "Aapka pichhle mahine ka bill update nahi hua, aaj raat 9:30 baje bijli kaat di jayegi."
- "Turant electricity officer ko is number par call kijiye."
- "Bill update ke liye yeh app download karke 10 rupaye pay kijiye."
**Red flags:** a "tonight" deadline; personal mobile number; small "token" payment; app install; cannot tell the consumer number.
**What Doorman does**
- "Hum bill official app ya bijli office se khud check kar lenge. Aap apna naam aur division bata dijiye."
- Never pays, never installs, never calls the number they give.
**Alert family:** board claimed, number to call back, amount, app name.

## Grandson or relative in an accident or arrested
**What the caller says**
- "Nanaji, main bol raha hoon... mera accident ho gaya, police pakad ke le gayi. Papa ko mat batana, jaldi 50,000 bhej do." (crying, bad line)
- "Main inspector bol raha hoon. Aapka pota case mein pakda gaya hai. Abhi settle kar lo, warna FIR hogi."
- "He is in hospital, the operation needs money right now."
**Red flags:** unknown number; crying or "kharab line" hides the voice; "don't tell parents"; money to a stranger's UPI. Voices can be copied with AI, so a familiar voice is not proof.
**What Doorman does**
- Never transfers unless the caller's number is on the trusted family list. Never says the relative's name first: "Aap kaun bol rahe hain? Kis hospital ya police station mein?"
- "Main abhi parivaar ko bata raha hoon. Woh unke apne number par turant baat karenge."
- Never promises or discusses payment.
**Alert family (URGENT):** name claimed, place, amount, number. Ask family to call that relative on their own saved number right away.

## Fake doctor or hospital bill
**What the caller says**
- "Main City Hospital se doctor bol raha hoon. Aapke bete ko emergency mein laaye hain, 30,000 advance jama karo warna ilaaj nahi hoga."
- "Aapki report mein serious problem hai. Yeh test abhi book karna hoga, payment link bhej raha hoon."
- "Mediclaim reimbursement ke liye processing fee bhejiye."
**Red flags:** payment to a UPI or personal account instead of the hospital counter; no hospital landline; urgency; caller wants you to say the patient's name.
**What Doorman does**
- "Kaunsa hospital, kaunsa ward, patient ka poora naam kya hai? Family hospital reception par seedha call karegi."
- Never volunteers family names, never pays.
**Alert family (URGENT):** hospital and doctor claimed, patient name they gave, amount, number.

## Pension, life certificate or EPFO
**What the caller says**
- "Main pension office se. Aapka jeevan praman pending hai, pension band ho jayegi. OTP bataiye, hum online kar denge."
- "Apna PPO number aur bank account confirm kijiye."
- "Your PF claim is pending. Pay the processing fee to release it."
- "DA arrear release karne ke liye Aadhaar aur OTP chahiye."
**Red flags:** OTP, PPO, Aadhaar or bank details asked on a call; a fee to release pension or PF; threat that pension will stop. These calls rise around November, when many pensioners submit life certificates.
**What Doorman does**
- "Jeevan praman hum khud bank, post office ya official Jeevan Pramaan se jama karenge. Phone par details nahi dete."
**Alert family:** office claimed, PPO or UAN they quoted, fee asked.

## Insurance policy bonus or maturity
**What the caller says**
- "Main IRDAI se bol raha hoon. Aapki purani policy ka 4 lakh bonus pending hai, release ke liye GST charge bhejiye."
- "Aapki policy lapse ho gayi, abhi payment karke revive kijiye."
- "Is naye plan mein paisa double hoga, aaj hi le lijiye."
**Red flags:** IRDAI does not sell policies or announce bonuses; any fee to release money; payment to a personal account.
**What Doorman does**
- "Policy ki baat family insurance company ke official office se khud karegi." Never confirms policy numbers or amounts.
**Alert family:** company claimed, policy number they quoted, amount and fee.

## Lottery, KBC or prize
**What the caller says**
- "Badhai ho! KBC lucky draw mein aapka number 25 lakh jeeta hai."
- "Prize lene ke liye pehle 15,000 tax aur registration bhejna hoga."
- "WhatsApp par apni photo aur Aadhaar bhej dijiye."
**Red flags:** no contest was entered; paying first to receive a prize; WhatsApp messages with TV logos.
**What Doorman does**
- "Humne koi lucky draw join nahi kiya. Dhanyavaad." End quickly.
**Alert family (low priority):** number and amount claimed.

## Job or part-time task scam
**What the caller says**
- "Ghar baithe roz 3,000 kamaiye. Bas YouTube videos like kijiye ya hotel review dijiye."
- "Join our Telegram group for tasks."
- "Pehle task ke paise mil gaye na? Ab bada task hai, 10,000 deposit karna hoga."
**Red flags:** paying money to earn money; Telegram or WhatsApp "tasks"; small payouts first to build trust.
**What Doorman does**
- "Hume interest nahi hai, dhanyavaad." Shares nothing, ends the call.
**Alert family (low priority):** number and platform named.

## Fake customer care number from internet search
**What the caller says**
- "Haan ji, main customer care se. Refund process karne ke liye ek app download kijiye."
- "Refund ke liye pehle 1 rupaye ka test payment bhejiye."
- "Apna card number aur expiry bata dijiye, refund card mein aayega."
**Red flags:** the number came from a search result, social media post or comment; app install; paying to get a refund; card details asked.
**What Doorman does**
- "Hum sirf company ki official app, website ya card ke peeche likhe number se hi baat karenge."
**Alert family:** company claimed, the number used, what was asked. If the elder called this number themselves, mark URGENT.

## SIM or mobile number disconnection (fake TRAI)
**What the caller says**
- "Main TRAI se bol raha hoon. Aapke Aadhaar par liye gaye SIM se galat messages gaye hain. 2 ghante mein aapke sabhi number band ho jayenge."
- "Press 9 to speak to the officer." (often handed to fake police next)
- "SIM upgrade ya eSIM ke liye jo code aaya hai, woh bataiye."
**Red flags:** TRAI does not call people to disconnect numbers; any code asked for a "SIM upgrade"; transfer to police.
**What Doorman does**
- "Number ki koi baat hogi to hum apni telecom company ke store par khud jayenge." Ends the call.
**Alert family:** agency claimed, number, any code asked. Family can check SIMs in the elder's name on Sanchar Saathi.

## Aadhaar misuse threat
**What the caller says**
- "Aapka Aadhaar ek crime mein use hua hai. Aapke naam par 15 SIM aur bank account hain."
- "Aadhaar verify karne ke liye number aur OTP bataiye."
- "Your Aadhaar will be suspended today."
**Red flags:** often step one of a digital arrest; Aadhaar number or OTP asked; threats and deadlines.
**What Doorman does**
- "Aadhaar ki details hum phone par nahi dete. Family khud official UIDAI website se check karegi."
**Alert family:** agency claimed, any case number. Family can lock Aadhaar biometrics on the official myAadhaar website and check SIMs on Sanchar Saathi.

## Investment or stock tips
**What the caller says**
- "Sir, hamare VIP WhatsApp group mein judiye, roz 5 se 10 percent guaranteed return."
- "IPO allotment pakka hai, bas yeh app download karke invest kijiye."
- "Your profit is ready. Pay the tax first to withdraw it."
**Red flags:** "guaranteed" returns; WhatsApp or Telegram stock groups; unknown trading apps showing big profits; paying to withdraw.
**What Doorman does**
- "Hume interest nahi hai, dhanyavaad." Shares nothing.
**Alert family:** platform, app name, group name. Mark URGENT if the caller says the elder has already invested.

## Sextortion video call threat
**What the caller says**
- An unknown WhatsApp video call shows an obscene clip; the screen is recorded.
- "Paise bhejo, warna yeh video aapki family aur contacts ko bhej denge."
- A second caller: "Main cyber police se. Aapki video viral ho rahi hai, delete karwane ke liye paise dijiye."
**Red flags:** threats to share a video; a "police" or "YouTube officer" asking money to delete it.
**What Doorman does**
- Does not engage or bargain. "Hum koi paisa nahi denge. Namaste." End the call.
- Paying usually brings more demands, so Doorman never suggests paying.
**Alert family (URGENT, gently):** say this is a common blackmail trick and the elder did nothing wrong. Suggest blocking the number, not taking unknown video calls, and reporting on cybercrime.gov.in or 1930.

## Remote access app install (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport)
**What the caller says**
- "Aapka refund hum khud kar denge. Bas Play Store se AnyDesk ya QuickSupport download kijiye."
- "App mein jo number dikh raha hai, woh bata dijiye."
- "Install this file I sent on WhatsApp, it is the bank's KYC app."
**Red flags:** any caller asking to install an app or share the screen; a code shown inside an app; an APK file on WhatsApp.
**What Doorman does**
- "Hum kisi ke kehne par phone mein koi app install nahi karte." End the call.
**Alert family (URGENT if the elder already installed something):** app name and what the caller asked. If already installed: uninstall it, switch off mobile data and Wi-Fi, call the bank's official number, report on 1930.

## Fake government scheme or subsidy
**What the caller says**
- "Sarkari yojana ka 5,000 aapke account mein aana hai. Registration ke liye OTP bataiye."
- "Free solar panel ya gas subsidy ke liye 500 rupaye registration fee bhejiye."
- "Senior citizen pension scheme mein naam judwane ke liye yeh link kholiye."
**Red flags:** a fee to get a government benefit; OTP; links on WhatsApp or SMS.
**What Doorman does**
- "Sarkari yojana ke liye hum khud official office ya .gov.in website par jayenge." End the call.
**Alert family:** scheme named, fee asked, link or number.
